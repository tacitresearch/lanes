| Uploads (`examples/upload`, a package of its own) | Uploads of any size over HTTP, each handed to the system in pieces of 64 KiB through the gate and answered with its size and Adler-32 checksum; the service model-checked on pieces in any order, and driven through the whole front end with no socket on bodies framed by their length and in chunks; `curl -T` of 20 MB answered with zlib's checksum | The system implements its spec (`Upload.implements`, by `Server.implements`); no more uploads are under way than the bound (`Upload.system_uploads_le`); the piece an upload waits for is taken and the next goes on from it (`Upload.step_piece`), and pieces taken one after another leave the size and the checksum of their bytes together (`Upload.take_take`, `Upload.start_take`) |
