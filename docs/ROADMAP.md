# Roadmap

The direction of Lanes is issue #93, and the work is the open issues; what is fixable and known
is labelled `known-limit`. This file only says where those are and what the order of work rests
on.

Lanes is for software an agent writes and nobody reads line by line, so its claims rest on
proofs, not on checks, tests or review (`docs/decisions/D45-everything-is-proved.md`). Work that
makes proving an application cheaper, gives every definition evidence, or shrinks what is
trusted and assumed comes before new features. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` says what exists, with
its claims and limits, and `docs/decisions/` why.
