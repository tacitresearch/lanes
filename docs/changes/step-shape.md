### Added

- A proposal for the step's final shape (`docs/decisions/new-step-shape.md`, #99): a step told the wall time, a seed derived from its position and a secret of the journal, and its sender (`Ctx`, with `Actor.Plain` for actors that need none of it, and the time and seed as a `tick` the environment chooses in the semantics and the checker); quarantine of a unit of work that killed two starts in a row, with `lanes journal quarantined` and `redrive`; a restart policy for actors that stop; a lint of the `!` operators; and aborting on a panic. It records what a compiled panic does in Lean v4.37: it prints, returns `default` and goes on, and `LEAN_ABORT_ON_PANIC`, set to any value, even `0`, makes it abort. No code changes yet. When the wall clock is adopted, existing journals' clocks jump from summed uptime to the wall clock at their first start, and pending timers fire at once.
